# Tata Motors — 5-Year Financial Performance & Valuation Analysis

A comprehensive financial analysis of Tata Motors covering FY2022–FY2026, focusing on financial performance, profitability, leverage, cash flow, operating performance and valuation.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Tata Motors' financial performance over a five-year period using consolidated financial data from the company's annual reports.

The objective is to understand how the company's:

- Revenue and profitability evolved
- EBITDA and EBIT margins changed
- Debt and leverage developed
- Operating cash flow and free cash flow performed
- Asset and equity base changed
- Earnings per share evolved
- Overall financial position changed
- Valuation can be interpreted from a fundamental perspective

## Research Objective

The primary objective is to answer:

> **How did Tata Motors' financial performance and financial position evolve between FY2022 and FY2026, and what does the analysis suggest about the company's underlying financial strength and valuation?**

## Analysis Period

**FY2022 – FY2026**

All major financial figures are analyzed on a consolidated basis where applicable.

## Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Revenue & Growth

Analysis of revenue trends across the five-year period, including changes in the company's scale and growth trajectory.

### 2. Profitability

Analysis of:

- EBITDA
- EBIT
- Profit Before Tax
- Net Profit
- EPS
- EBITDA Margin
- EBIT Margin
- Net Profit Margin

### 3. Balance Sheet

Analysis of:

- Total Assets
- Total Equity
- Total Debt
- Cash & Cash Equivalents
- Debt-to-Equity
- Changes in financial leverage

### 4. Cash Flow

Analysis of:

- Operating Cash Flow
- Capital Expenditure
- Free Cash Flow
- Cash generation relative to accounting profits

### 5. Operating Performance

The analysis also considers vehicle sales and operating indicators where reliable data is available.

### 6. Valuation

The project examines valuation from a fundamental perspective using relevant financial metrics and business performance indicators.
